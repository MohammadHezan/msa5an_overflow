# Jordan regulations on shared / pooled / multi-request ride-hailing trips (2025–2026), and what they mean for a student ride-sharing feature

Research date: 27 Nov 2026. About 15 searches and fetches, Arabic and English sources. The Official Gazette text of the 2025 regulation could not be retrieved (al-Mamlaka returned 403, the intaj.net PDF returned 404, the Roya page was empty). Everything below about specific articles comes from law-firm summaries or news reports, not the primary text.

## Q1. What does the reported Nov 2026 rule ban regarding shared / multi-request trips?

### Takeaway
I could not confirm any Nov 2026 Petra or Ammon report, bylaw article, or LTRC decision that specifically bans shared or pooled trips where one car carries several unrelated passenger requests. The confirmed 2025–2026 framework is about licensing, fares, permits, data links and blocking unlicensed apps. The closest thing to a "ban" is an LTRC statement (Nov 2026) that organising trips through WhatsApp groups or social-media pages is a violation.

### Cited Findings
- **The governing bylaw:** "نظام تنظيم نقل الركاب من خلال استخدام التطبيقات الذكية" (Regulation on Passenger Transport Using Smart Applications) of 2025, issued under Passenger Transport Regulation Law No. 19 of 2017. It replaces the 2018 regulation. A Nov 2025 law-firm summary gives its number as **No. (74) of 2025**. — [Nsair & Partners, 13 Nov 2025](https://nsairs.com/2025/11/13/%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%AE%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8-2/). **Conflict:** a search-result snippet citing JFRA News says the 2025 implementing instructions were issued under Article 30 of regulation "رقم 47 لسنة 2025" (No. 47 of 2025) — [JFRA News](https://jfranews.com.jo/article/521031), page not fetched. The number has to be checked against the Gazette.
- **Effective date:** the regulation took effect on **16 October 2025**. Quote: "أصبح نظام تنظيم نقل الركاب من خلال استخدام التطبيقات الذكية نافذًا اعتبارًا من تاريخ 16 تشرين الأول 2025" ("the regulation became effective as of 16 October 2025"). — [Ammon News, 18 Nov 2026](https://www.ammonnews.net/article/1046001)
- **Core prohibition (as reported):** no one may carry passengers through any app without both a licence and a permit. — [Ammon News, 18 Nov 2026](https://www.ammonnews.net/article/1046001)
- **Article summaries (from the Nsair summary of Reg. 74/2025):**
  - **Art. 4(a)(2):** JOD 100,000 bank guarantee per company. Art. 4(c) exempts taxi offices from it.
  - **Art. 5(b):** licensees may not use their databases "لغير غاية نقل الركاب من خلال استخدام التطبيقات الذكية" ("for any purpose other than passenger transport via smart apps").
  - **Art. 5(ك)/(ل):** vehicles set aside for people with disabilities.
  - **Art. 6:** data-sharing and electronic-link contract with the LTRC.
  - **Art. 10:** maximum driver age 65, plus mandatory training.
  - **Art. 15:** sanctions.
  - **Art. 18:** the LTRC may ask the Telecom Regulatory Commission "لحجب أي تطبيق أو موقع إلكتروني" ("to block any app or website") that offers unlicensed transport.
  - **Art. 19:** six-month transition period for existing operators.
  - The summary has no provision on shared or pooled trips. — [Nsair & Partners, 13 Nov 2025](https://nsairs.com/2025/11/13/%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%AE%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8-2/)
- **Another 2025 summary, same gap:** a separate 2025 law-firm summary covers vehicles of 7 years or less, drivers under 65 who own the car or whose close relative owns it, LTRC-approved fare and commission structures, and blocking of unlicensed apps. It also has no shared-trip provision. — [Jaradat Law, 2025](https://jaradatlaw.com/ar/legislative-update-regulation-of-ride-hailing-and-smart-transport-services-in-jordan-2025/)
- **The 2018 predecessor:** it defined the service as "نقل الركاب مقابل أجر من خلال استخدام التطبيقات الذكية" ("carrying passengers for a fee via smart apps"). It did not define "trip" or "passenger" and had no shared-trip clause. — [JordanianLaw.com (2018 text)](https://jordanianlaw.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%85%D8%A9/%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%AE%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7/)
- **WhatsApp and social-media trips (Nov 2026):** the LTRC official in charge of smart apps said there are 8 licensed apps against more than 12–13 unlicensed ones. He said organising trips through WhatsApp groups or social-media pages is a violation, and that such pages keep reappearing under new names. — Reported by [Jo24](https://jo24.net/article/583646), [Watana News](https://watananews.com/573590/) and [Twasl News](https://twaslnews1.twaslnews.com/8138252/), around 22 Nov 2026 (taken from search-result summaries). Ammon adds that unlicensed operators "ينقلون الحجوزات إلى «واتساب» والخطوط الهاتفية" ("move bookings to WhatsApp and phone lines"). — [Ammon News, 18 Nov 2026](https://www.ammonnews.net/article/1046001)
- **Other 2026 LTRC actions (none about pooling):**
  - Fare cap of the taxi meter rate plus at most 20%.
  - JOD 50,000 of the 100,000 guarantee is cashed in on a violation.
  - Mystery-shopper inspection campaign from 7 Oct 2026, covering Uber, Careem, Jeeny and Petra Ride.
  - Sources: [Jo24](https://jo24.net/article/578751), [Garaa News](https://garaanews.com/article/406568), [Jordan News](https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-109/News/Jordan-Transport-Authority-Tightens-App-Based-Taxi-Oversight-56875)
- **"Release" feature (June 2026):** the LTRC denied it would cancel the feature that lets a driver work on several apps. — [Jordan News](https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-109/News/Land-Transport-Regulatory-Commission-No-Decision-to-Cancel-the-Release-Feature-in-Ride-Hailing-Apps-52099)

### Inferences
- **The premise may be wrong.** The "Nov 2026 shared-trip ban" in the brief may conflate the Nov 2026 coverage (unlicensed apps, WhatsApp-group trips, 8 licensed apps) with a pooling ban. I found no wording that prohibits a licensed app from matching multiple requests.
- **Pooling may still need approval.** Because fares and fare structures must be LTRC-approved (Art. 5 and the fare instructions), a per-seat or pooled fare model would likely need LTRC approval even if pooling isn't expressly banned. This is my reading, not a confirmed rule.
- **The real exposure is licensing.** Any platform that matches paying passengers with drivers falls under "نقل الركاب مقابل أجر" (carrying passengers for a fee) and needs a licence, a JOD 100k guarantee and permitted drivers. That is the main risk for a student pooling feature run by an unlicensed app.

### Gaps
- Full Official Gazette text of Reg. 74 (or 47) of 2025 and the 2025 instructions, especially the definitions article and any clause on "أكثر من راكب/طلب" (more than one passenger or request). I couldn't retrieve it.
- Whether the Petra or Ammon article the brief refers to exists. Searches of Petra and Ammon for Nov 2026 found no pooling-ban article.

## Q2. Can one booker book a private ride for friends and split the cost? Is in-app splitting allowed?

### Takeaway
Nothing I found prohibits a single booker from booking one private ride for a group, which is the normal licensed service, or splitting the cost among themselves. No source addresses in-app fare splitting either way.

### Cited Findings
- Licensees must submit a clear fare-calculation method for LTRC approval. The 2018 Art. 5(د) wording is "تقديم أسس وآلية احتساب واضحة لأجور نقل الركاب" ("submit clear bases and a calculation method for passenger fares"). — [JordanianLaw.com (2018 text)](https://jordanianlaw.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%85%D8%A9/%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%AE%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7/). The 2025 regime keeps fare and commission approval. — [Jaradat Law](https://jaradatlaw.com/ar/legislative-update-regulation-of-ride-hailing-and-smart-transport-services-in-jordan-2025/)
- The 2026 fare cap is the taxi meter rate plus at most 20%, and violations cost JOD 50k of the guarantee. — [Garaa News](https://garaanews.com/article/406568), [Jo24](https://jo24.net/article/578751)

### Inferences
- **Lowest risk:** one booking, one fare at or under the cap, and friends reimbursing each other outside the ride platform (a payment or expense-split feature). That doesn't change the trip or the fare.
- **Possibly fine:** in-app splitting of a single approved fare doesn't seem to alter the fare. The risk would come from per-seat pricing that differs from the approved fare.

### Gaps
- No explicit rule or LTRC statement on fare splitting. Careem and Uber split-fare availability in Jordan was not verified.

## Q3. Are university shuttles, scheduled group rides, or non-commercial student carpools treated differently?

### Takeaway
No source addresses these categories under the smart-app regime. The framework covers transport "for a fee" through apps, and the LTRC treats trips organised via WhatsApp or social media as violations. Paid student carpools coordinated through an app or group chat are therefore likely in scope. Purely cost-free sharing is not addressed.

### Cited Findings
- The regulated activity is carrying passengers for a fee via smart apps (2018 Art. 2). Art. 3 prohibits it without a licence and permit. — [JordanianLaw.com](https://jordanianlaw.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%85%D8%A9/%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%AE%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7/)
- Law No. 19 of 2017 prohibits anyone from providing passenger transport services without LTRC registration and licensing. — [Al Tamimi & Co.](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update-articles/ride-hailing-apps-in-jordan/)
- WhatsApp and social-media trip organising is a violation (LTRC, around 22 Nov 2026). — [Jo24](https://jo24.net/article/583646)
- A self-described "community-driven carpooling app designed for Jordan" (Ridezy) exists on the App Store. I found no evidence it holds an LTRC licence. — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/kw/app/ridezy/id6670398528)

### Inferences
- **Paid peer carpooling is risky.** A student app where drivers who are not LTRC-permitted receive money from passengers looks legally like unlicensed passenger transport.
- **Safer alternatives:** (a) partner with a licensed app such as Jeeny or Careem for group or event rides, (b) have the university run its own licensed bus or shuttle (a separate regime), or (c) do pure coordination with no payment to the driver. Option (c) is untested legally.

### Gaps
- No source found on university-shuttle licensing, scheduled group rides, or whether cost-sharing without profit counts as "for a fee".

## Q4. Rules on women-only or female-driver options

### Takeaway
I found no 2025–2026 provision that requires or prohibits women-only or female-driver options. Driver eligibility rules are gender-neutral. Women-only taxi services have existed in Jordan before.

### Cited Findings
- The 2018 Art. 6 eligibility conditions (Jordanian nationality, vehicle ownership by self or relative, and so on) contain no gender clause. — [JordanianLaw.com](https://jordanianlaw.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%85%D8%A9/%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%AE%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7/). The 2025 summaries also mention none. — [Nsair](https://nsairs.com/2025/11/13/%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%AE%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8-2/), [Jaradat](https://jaradatlaw.com/ar/legislative-update-regulation-of-ride-hailing-and-smart-transport-services-in-jordan-2025/)
- **Historical precedents (older, undated in my notes):**
  - "Pink taxis" in Amman: a fleet of about 10 women-driven cars for female passengers, whose riders included university students. — [Gulf News/AFP](https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/world%2Fmena%2Fwomen-only-taxi-service-challenges-norms-in-jordan-1.1953992), [Arab News PK](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/1031661)
  - The "Shecab" women-only taxi concept, around 2014. — [Jordan Times](https://jordantimes.com/news/local/projected-female-exclusive-taxi-service-looks-offer-safe-transport-job-opportunities)

### Inferences
- A female-driver preference filter isn't barred by anything found, but the drivers would still need LTRC permits.

### Gaps
- Whether any licensed app in Jordan currently offers a women-only product. Not verified.

## Q5. Jeeny's status in Jordan

### Takeaway
Jeeny (operated in Jordan by Bab Al Qamar Co.) is one of the LTRC-recognised licensed apps in 2026. In July 2025 it became Al-Ahliyya Amman University's official transport partner, offering event-based discount promo codes. No group, scheduled or shuttle product was described.

### Cited Findings
- The LTRC named Jeeny alongside Uber, Careem and Petra Ride as licensed companies at its oversight meeting. The inspection campaign started 7 Oct 2026. — [Jo24](https://jo24.net/article/578751), [Jordan News](https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-109/News/Jordan-Transport-Authority-Tightens-App-Based-Taxi-Oversight-56875)
- Jeeny was among the companies (Careem, Uber, Petra Ride, Jeeny, Taxi F) ordered to apply the new 2026 fare within a week. — [Garaa News](https://garaanews.com/article/406568)
- **AAU and Bab Al Qamar (Jeeny) MoU, July 2025:** covers practical training and digital skills. It was signed by AAU President Prof. Sari Hamdan and Jeeny SVP Marketing and Brand Amanda Yasmineh. — [AAU news](https://www.ammanu.edu.jo/news/al-ahliyya-amman-university-signs-cooperation-agreement-with-bab-al-qamar-jeeny-to-enhance-practical-training-and-digital-skills-for-students/)
- **Official transport partner (updated 23 Jul 2025):**
  - "exclusive discounts on campus rides and university activities"
  - discounted rides to and from AAU during designated events (Open Day, registration, job fairs, concerts)
  - promo codes posted through AAU channels
  - no discount rate, duration, shuttles or group rides stated
  - Source: [Jordan News](https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-33/Trade-Industry/Jeeny-Becomes-Official-Transport-Partner-for-AAU-to-Power-Affordable-Student-Mobility-43445)

### Inferences
- The Jeeny–AAU deal is a precedent for the compliant model: a licensed app plus a university promo partnership, with no pooling.

### Gaps
- Jeeny's licence date and number, and whether it has any scheduled or group ride product in Jordan. Not found.
