Friends ride in one car, even after today’s cars are planned.

Up to four students book together with a group code. Each of them pays only for the kilometres they ride, and together they pay exactly one regulated car fare. Try it on the phone: start a group, add friends, then find your car.

  • 1Ride options follow your genderA man sees Anyone or Men only. A woman sees Anyone or Women only.
  • 2The group’s option is its creator’sA men-only group refuses women, and a women-only group refuses men.
  • 3Friends are never splitThe group gets its car when the creator taps “find our car”, when four have joined, or after 10 minutes.
  • 4Open or closedAn open group may take free seats in a car that is already planned, only if every rule still passes. A closed group always gets a car of its own.
